# Acceptable Use Policy

## Prohibited Content

The following content is strictly prohibited:

1. Sexual content involving minors (CSAM) — zero tolerance, reported to authorities
2. Content designed to facilitate violence against specific individuals
3. Illegal weapons manufacturing instructions
4. Malware, ransomware, or exploit code
5. Content designed to defraud individuals or organizations
6. Mass harassment campaigns
7. Misinformation designed to cause public harm

## Automated Use

- Automated API usage is permitted within rate limits
- Bots must identify themselves in User-Agent or system prompt
- Scraping our platform or reverse-engineering our systems is prohibited
- Selling access to your account credentials is prohibited

## Enforcement

Violations result in immediate account suspension without refund.
Severe violations (CSAM, explicit threats) are reported to appropriate authorities.

## Reporting

Report violations to: abuse@yourplatform.com
